# Evercanon — brand and product principles

## Name

**Evercanon**

## Tagline

*Nothing is final. Everything leaves a trace.*

## Supporting line

**One world. Infinite versions. Written by everyone. Shaped by collective choice. Erased by no one.**

## Homepage seed copy

> In the beginning, in the yard of angels, there were twelve dwarves and one irrational number.

Every word can change. Every change creates another possibility. The world you see is the one we chose together—for now.

## Meaning

“Canon” is the version accepted as the official story; “ever” implies that the accepted version can keep changing. A popular branch becomes the default reading path, but it does not erase competing branches.

## Product principles

1. **Popularity determines visibility, never existence.** A low-ranked story remains readable and extendable unless a separate, auditable safety/legal moderation decision restricts public display.
2. **History is part of the work.** Accepted contributions are never silently overwritten.
3. **Collective authorship, lightweight participation.** No account is required for the MVP. Anonymous identity is pseudonymous and abuse controls should be proportionate.
4. **The interface stays readable.** The preferred story reads like a story. Hover, tap, or keyboard focus reveals alternatives at the relevant word, phrase, sentence, or branch.
5. **No false finality.** Rankings are a current default, not a claim that one branch is permanently correct.

## Suggested interface language

- “Explore another reality”
- “Propose an alternative”
- “This version is currently preferred”
- “Every version remains part of the history”
- “Vote on this version”

Avoid “deleted forever,” “the true version,” or language implying popularity proves correctness.
